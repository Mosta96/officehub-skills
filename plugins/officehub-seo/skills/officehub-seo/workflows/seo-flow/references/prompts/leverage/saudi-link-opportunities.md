# Saudi Link and Mention Opportunities

## Use This When

The store's pages are in good shape but competitors with weaker pages outrank it, or AI
assistants name competitors and not OfficeHub.

## Inputs

- `references/officehub-profile.md` (brands carried, B2B clients you may name, cities).
- 3–5 competitor domains.
- Optional: backlink export (Ahrefs/Semrush/GSC Links) for officehub.sa and competitors.

## Prompt

```text
Find realistic link and brand-mention opportunities for officehub.sa, a Saudi
office-furniture store. Only earned or partner links. No paid links, PBNs or link
exchanges.

Look for, with web search:
1. Brand/manufacturer "where to buy" or dealer pages for brands the store carries.
2. Fit-out contractors, interior designers and architects in Saudi Arabia who publish
   projects and could credit the furniture supplier.
3. B2B clients whose office projects could become a case study (with permission) on
   both sites.
4. Saudi business, real-estate and workplace media writing about new offices,
   coworking and workplace wellbeing (ergonomics) where an expert quote fits.
5. Chambers of commerce, trade exhibitions and supplier directories that list members.
6. Unlinked mentions of OfficeHub (Arabic and English spellings).
7. Pages that link to 2+ competitors but not to OfficeHub (if a backlink export is given).

For each opportunity give: site, why it is relevant, the asset or reason to link
(case study, expert quote, dealer listing), and a one-line outreach angle in Arabic.
Mark any site you could not verify as live and indexed.
```

## Output

- Opportunity table, prioritized by relevance and effort.
- Assets the store needs to create (case studies, expert quotes, spec sheets).
